# Sebastian: 2 minute demo

Two phones: the **Redmi** (you, Sebastian runs here) and the **Vigotel** (your "partner", Shahwaiz in the story). Film the Redmi's screen and your face; put the Vigotel on speaker next to the camera so the audience hears both sides.

Tips
- Say "Sebastian" clearly at the start of a question. Pause half a second after he answers before talking again.
- If a take goes wrong, just hang up and call again: every call starts fresh.
- Keep the room quiet; the Vigotel on speaker is fine, the Redmi is better on earpiece.
